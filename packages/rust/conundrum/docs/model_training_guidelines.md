# Model Training Guidelines

1. The internal model must be trained on Conundrum content. HTML should be converted to Conundrum, images should have text extracted to markdown, etc.
2. Training should be carried out once a year while funds allow, driven completely by the community. There should exist an open-source repository who's function is to collect training data, convert it to Conundrum or Markdown, and train or fine-tune the model accordingly.
3. As elsewhere in the Conundrum ecosystem, the AI training repository should be managed by AI, as to remove human bias.
