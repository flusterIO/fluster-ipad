Mueller) haven't had a paycheck in almost 5 years and am still borderline homeless, any _paid_ services must receive a written aggreement from myself prior to use of Conundrum, up until I make 80k/year, from any source, at which point the entire Conundrum ecosystem will adopt an MIT license.

In short, if you want to build upon the Conundrum ecosystem, that's great, but it's going to have to be free & open source until I can get myself a normal income again, at which point you can do anything (legal) that you want with Conundrum
